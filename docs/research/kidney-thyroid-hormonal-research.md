## KIDNEY DOMAIN

### 1. eGFR
- **Measures:** estimated kidney filtration (from creatinine ± cystatin C + age/sex, CKD-EPI).
- **Why it matters:** primary CKD detection/staging marker; eGFR <60 for ≥3 months defines CKD.
- **Unit:** mL/min/1.73 m².
- **Normal (NKF, verified):** ≥90. Average declines with age (116 at 20–29 → 75 at 70+). Not sex-specific.
- **KDIGO stages (verified via NKF):** G1 ≥90 · G2 60–89 · G3a 45–59 · G3b 30–44 · G4 15–29 · G5 <15. (G1/G2 = CKD only with kidney damage, e.g., uACR ≥30, ≥3 months.)
- **Sources:** NKF eGFR — https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/estimated-glomerular-filtration-rate-egfr ; NKF CKD stages — https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/stages-chronic-kidney-disease-ckd ; NIDDK — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/professionals/clinical-tools-patient-management/kidney-disease/laboratory-evaluation ; provenance KDIGO 2024 (kdigo.org 403).

### 2. Serum Creatinine
- **Measures:** muscle-metabolism waste cleared by kidneys; eGFR input.
- **Unit:** mg/dL (SI µmol/L ×88.4).
- **Reference (sex-specific, CONFLICTS recorded):**
  - MedlinePlus (verified): **M 0.7–1.3 · F 0.5–0.95 mg/dL**
  - StatPearls NBK544228: M ~0.6–1.2 · F 0.5–1.1
  - Clinical Methods NBK305: M 0.6–1.2 (enzymatic) · F 0.5–1.1
- **No creatinine-based staging (staging via eGFR).**
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003475.htm ; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK544228/ ; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK305/

### 3. BUN
- **Measures:** urea nitrogen (protein breakdown product).
- **Unit:** mg/dL (5–20 mg/dL ≈ 1.8–7.1 mmol/L urea).
- **Reference (CONFLICT recorded):** MedlinePlus **6–20** vs Clinical Methods **5–20 mg/dL**. Not sex-specific. No staging.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003474.htm ; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK305/

### 4. Uric Acid
- **Measures:** purine metabolism end-product.
- **Why it matters:** hyperuricemia → gout, urate stones; kidney disease association.
- **Unit:** mg/dL (6.8 mg/dL ≈ 400 µmol/L).
- **Reference (MedlinePlus, verified, sex-specific):** **F 3.0–7.1 · M 4.0–8.6 mg/dL**.
- **Thresholds (StatPearls, verified):** hyperuricemia >6 (F) / >7 (M) mg/dL; solubility threshold 6.8 mg/dL (sex-independent); gout risk ↑ ≥8; flare risk ↑ >9. Gout treatment target <6 (or <5) mg/dL (MedlinePlus).
- **CONFLICT noted:** lab reference upper bounds (7.1/8.6) sit above clinical hyperuricemia thresholds — reference interval ≠ clinical threshold; present both.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003476.htm ; StatPearls NBK459218 (upd. 2023-10-14); NBK546606.

## THYROID DOMAIN

### 5. TSH
- **Measures:** pituitary thyrotropin; most sensitive first-line thyroid index.
- **Unit:** mIU/L (= mU/L = µU/mL).
- **Reference (CONFLICTS recorded, all listed):**
  - MedlinePlus (verified): 0.4–4.8 µU/mL
  - StatPearls NBK499850 (verified): 0.4–4.5 mU/L
  - USPSTF review NBK285870 (verified): 0.45–4.5 mIU/L
  - **ATA 2014 guideline (verified via PMC full text): 0.4–4.0 mIU/L therapeutic target**
- **Thresholds:** subclinical hypothyroidism = high TSH + normal FT4; treatment generally when TSH >10 mIU/L (StatPearls NBK536970). TSH runs higher in age >80 without disease.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003684.htm ; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK499850/ ; https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK285870/ ; ATA/Jonklaas 2014 — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4267409/ ; NBK536970.

### 6. Free T4
- **Unit:** ng/dL (SI pmol/L).
- **Reference (CONFLICT recorded):** MedlinePlus (verified) **0.8–1.9 ng/dL** (10.3–24.5 pmol/L) vs Cleveland Clinic (verified) 0.9–1.7 ng/dL.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003517.htm ; https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diagnostics/24235-thyroxine-t4-test

### 7. Free T3
- **Unit:** pg/mL (MedlinePlus expresses pg/dL; 210–440 pg/dL = 2.1–4.4 pg/mL).
- **Reference (CONFLICT recorded):** MedlinePlus (verified) **2.1–4.4 pg/mL** (3.2–6.8 pmol/L) vs Cleveland Clinic (verified) 2.3–4.1 pg/mL. (Total T3: 78–158 ng/dL MedlinePlus / 79–165 CC.)
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003687.htm ; https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diagnostics/22425-triiodothyronine-t3

### 8. Anti-TPO
- **Measures:** IgG autoantibodies vs thyroid peroxidase (autoimmune thyroid disease → Hashimoto).
- **Unit:** IU/mL.
- **Cutoff: STRONGLY LAB-DEPENDENT (record as lab-supplied):**
  - Cleveland Clinic (verified): **<5.6 IU/mL** negative
  - Mayo Labs test 81765: <9.0 IU/mL — **403-blocked, search-verified only; re-verify**
  - MedlinePlus/UCSF (verified): negative-vs-positive per lab; no universal numeric cutoff.
- **No guideline universal threshold exists.**
- **Sources:** https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diagnostics/thyroid-antibodies ; https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/thyroid-antibodies/ ; https://www.ucsfhealth.org/care/medical-tests/thyroid-peroxidase-antibody ; https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/81765 (blocked).

## HORMONAL DOMAIN

### 9. Cortisol (serum, morning)
- **Measures:** adrenal glucocorticoid; TIME-OF-DAY DEPENDENT (peak early morning, nadir ~midnight).
- **Unit:** µg/dL (SI nmol/L ×27.6).
- **Reference (CONFLICT recorded):** MedlinePlus 8 a.m. (verified): **5–25 µg/dL** (138–690 nmol/L) vs Cleveland Clinic 6–8 a.m. (verified): 10–20 µg/dL (4 p.m.: 3–10).
- **No single-value diagnostic categories** — Cushing/adrenal insufficiency need dynamic testing. Capture draw time.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003693.htm ; https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diagnostics/22417-cortisol-test

### 10. Testosterone (total)
- **Unit:** ng/dL (SI nmol/L ×0.0347).
- **Reference (MedlinePlus, verified, age+sex-specific):** M 17–50y: **249–836** · M >50y: 193–740 · F 17–50y: 8.4–48.1 · F >50y: 2.9–40.1 ng/dL.
- **Endocrine Society harmonized (verified, endocrine.org):** non-obese men 19–39: **264–916 ng/dL**.
- **Low-T thresholds (CONFLICT recorded, both listed):**
  - **AUA guideline (verified): <300 ng/dL** cutoff supporting diagnosis (2 early-morning draws + symptoms)
  - Endocrine Society 2018: 264 ng/dL lower limit (guideline use search-verified; harmonized range fetch-verified)
- **Sources:** AUA — https://www.auanet.org/guidelines-and-quality/guidelines/testosterone-deficiency-guideline ; Endocrine Society — https://www.endocrine.org/news-and-advocacy/news-room/2017/landmark-study-defines-normal-ranges-for-testosterone-levels ; MedlinePlus — https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003707.htm

### 11. Estradiol (E2)
- **Unit:** pg/mL (SI pmol/L ×3.67).
- **Reference (MedlinePlus, verified, context-dependent):** Males **10–50 pg/mL** · Premenopausal F **30–400 pg/mL** · Postmenopausal F **0–30 pg/mL**.
- **Menstrual-phase-specific ranges: Needs Clarification** — no fetchable authoritative universal phase table (Mayo catalog phase values 403-blocked/unverified; StatPearls NBK549797 has no numbers). Display the 3 verified contexts; phase interpretation = lab-report-dependent.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003711.htm ; Mayo EEST (blocked) — https://www.mayocliniclabs.com/test-catalog/overview/81816

## ADJUSTMENT FACTORS

### 12. BMI
- **Formula:** kg/m² (US: lb×703/in²).
- **Categories (verified WHO + MedlinePlus; CDC search-verified, consistent):** Underweight <18.5 · Healthy 18.5–24.9 · Overweight 25–29.9 · Obesity ≥30 (CDC classes: 1: 30–<35 · 2: 35–<40 · 3: ≥40).
- **Sources:** WHO — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/obesity-and-overweight ; MedlinePlus — https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/007196.htm ; CDC — https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/adult-calculator/bmi-categories.html (403; search-verified).

### 13. Physical Activity (WHO 2020, verified verbatim)
- Adults 18–64: **"at least 150–300 min of moderate-intensity aerobic physical activity, or at least 75–150 min of vigorous-intensity"** weekly (or equivalent combination) + muscle-strengthening ≥2 days/week. Insufficiently active = <150 min moderate/week.
- **Sources:** WHO 2020 guidelines (PMC7719906, verified) — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7719906/ ; WHO fact sheet — https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity ; publication — https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240015128

### 14. Smoking Status (CDC NHIS, verified verbatim from CDC archive)
- **Current smoker:** ≥100 lifetime cigarettes + currently smokes (every-day/some-day subtypes).
- **Former smoker:** ≥100 lifetime cigarettes, quit at interview.
- **Never smoker:** never smoked or <100 lifetime cigarettes.
- **Source:** CDC/NCHS NHIS Tobacco Glossary — https://archive.cdc.gov/www_cdc_gov/nchs/nhis/tobacco/tobacco_glossary.htm (live cdc.gov 403; archive fetched).
