> Research pass: 2026-08-18. ODS pages fetched directly (403 to default fetchers); WHO values extracted from official guideline PDFs (IRIS). All numbers read from source text, not memory.

## NUTRITIONAL DOMAIN

### 1. Vitamin D (25-hydroxyvitamin D)
- **Measures:** serum 25(OH)D — main circulating form; accepted status indicator (t½ ≈ 15 days).
- **Why it matters:** deficiency → impaired Ca absorption, rickets/osteomalacia; excess >150 nmol/L → toxicity risk.
- **Units:** ng/mL, nmol/L. **1 ng/mL = 2.5 nmol/L** (ODS Table 1 footnote).
- **NIH ODS/FNB categories (verified exact):** Deficiency **<12 ng/mL** (<30 nmol/L) · Inadequacy **12–<20** (30–<50) · Adequate **≥20** (≥50) · Potential adverse **>50 ng/mL** (>125 nmol/L, esp. >60/150).
- **Endocrine Society (CONFLICT — report both, label by year):**
  - 2011 Holick guideline: deficiency <20 · insufficiency 21–29 · sufficiency 30–100 ng/mL.
  - **2024 Demay guideline REPLACES 2011: no numeric thresholds endorsed; suggests against routine testing.** ODS corroborates.
- **Sources:** ODS Vitamin D HP fact sheet — https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminD-HealthProfessional/ ; Holick 2011 doi:10.1210/jc.2011-0385 ; Demay 2024 doi:10.1210/clinem/dgae290 — https://www.endocrine.org/clinical-practice-guidelines/vitamin-d-for-prevention-of-disease

### 2. Vitamin B12 (Cobalamin)
- **Measures:** serum B12 (DNA synthesis, RBC formation, neuro function).
- **Why it matters:** deficiency → megaloblastic anemia + potentially irreversible neuro damage.
- **Units:** pg/mL (SI pmol/L; 200 pg/mL = 148 pmol/L).
- **Thresholds (CONFLICT — report both):**
  - ODS (verified): subnormal **<200–250 pg/mL** (lab/method-dependent); gray zone 150–399 pg/mL → confirm with MMA >0.271 µmol/L; homocysteine >15 µmol/L supportive.
  - MedlinePlus (verified): normal **299–1054 pg/mL**; <299 possible deficiency.
- **Sources:** https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/VitaminB12-HealthProfessional/ ; https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003705.htm

### 3. Folate (serum)
- **Measures:** recent folate intake (RBC folate = long-term).
- **Why it matters:** deficiency → megaloblastic anemia, ↑homocysteine; low folate in reproductive-age women → NTD risk.
- **Units:** ng/mL (WHO factor: ×2.265 = nmol/L).
- **Thresholds (MULTIPLE — all listed):**
  - ODS: adequacy **>3 ng/mL** serum (RBC >140 ng/mL).
  - WHO 2015 (verified from PDF): hematologic deficiency **<3 ng/mL** (<6.8 nmol/L) · possible 3–5.9 · normal 6–20 · elevated >20; metabolic (homocysteine) deficiency **<4 ng/mL** (<10 nmol/L); NTD population sufficiency RBC folate **>400 ng/mL** (>906 nmol/L).
  - MedlinePlus: normal **2.7–17.0 ng/mL**.
- **Sources:** https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Folate-HealthProfessional/ ; WHO/NMH/NHD/EPG/15.01 — https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/WHO-NMH-NHD-EPG-15.01 ; https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003686.htm

### 4. Ferritin
- **Measures:** iron-storage protein; ∝ body iron stores (absent inflammation). Acute-phase reactant caveat.
- **Units:** ng/mL = µg/L (identical).
- **Reference (MedlinePlus, sex-specific):** M **30–400** · F **13–150 ng/mL**.
- **WHO 2020 thresholds (verified from PDF):** iron deficiency (healthy adults/adolescents/children ≥5y) **<15 µg/L** (<12 for under-5s; pregnancy T1 <15); with infection/inflammation **<30 (children) / <70 (adults) µg/L**; overload risk (healthy adults) **>150 (menstruating women) / >200 (men, non-menstruating women) µg/L** (>500 in non-healthy).
- **ODS clinical (CONFLICT with WHO — list both):** **<30 µg/L** suggests deficiency; **<10 µg/L** suggests IDA.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003490.htm ; WHO 2020 — https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240000124 ; https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-HealthProfessional/

### 5. Magnesium (serum)
- **Measures:** serum Mg (<1% of body Mg — imperfect status marker, ODS caveat).
- **Why it matters:** cofactor for 300+ enzymes; hypomagnesemia → neuromuscular/cardiac disturbance.
- **Units:** mg/dL (SI mmol/L; 1 mmol/L ≈ 2.43 mg/dL).
- **Reference (CONFLICT — both listed):** ODS **0.75–0.95 mmol/L** (hypomagnesemia <0.75) vs MedlinePlus **1.7–2.2 mg/dL** (0.70–0.91 mmol/L).
- **Sources:** https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Magnesium-HealthProfessional/ ; https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003487.htm

### 6. Zinc (serum)
- **Measures:** serum/plasma Zn — most common proxy (no gold standard; varies with time of day, inflammation).
- **Units:** µg/dL (SI µmol/L).
- **Reference (ODS, verified):** healthy **80–120 µg/dL** (12–18 µmol/L). Inadequate: **<70 (F) / <74 (M) µg/dL**.
- **STATUS: single-source anchor** — no WHO/MedlinePlus cross-check found; flag when displaying.
- **Source:** https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Zinc-HealthProfessional/

## HEMATOLOGICAL DOMAIN

### 7. Hemoglobin
- **Measures:** O₂-carrying protein concentration; defines anemia.
- **Units:** g/dL (SI g/L ×10).
- **Reference (MedlinePlus, sex-specific):** M **13.8–17.2** · F **12.1–15.1 g/dL**.
- **WHO 2024 anemia cutoffs (verified from guideline PDF):** M 15–65y **<13 g/dL** · non-pregnant F 15–65y **<12 g/dL** · pregnancy T1 <11, T2 <10.5, T3 <11 g/dL · children by age (105–120 g/L).
- **WHO severity (adults):** M mild 110–129 · moderate 80–109 · severe <80 g/L; F mild 110–119 · moderate 80–109 · severe <80 g/L.
- **Sources:** WHO 2024 — https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240088542 ; https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003645.htm

### 8. MCV
- **Measures:** average RBC volume → anemia classification.
- **Unit:** fL.
- **Reference (CONFLICT — both listed):** StatPearls **80–100 fL** (microcytic <80, macrocytic >100 — standard clinical classification) vs MedlinePlus **79–95 fL**.
- **Sources:** StatPearls NBK545275 — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK545275/ ; https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003648.htm

### 9. WBC
- **Measures:** total leukocytes.
- **Units:** cells/µL (SI ×10⁹/L).
- **Reference (MedlinePlus + StatPearls agree):** **4,500–11,000/µL (4.5–11.0 ×10⁹/L)**. Leukopenia <4.5 · leukocytosis >11.0 ×10⁹/L · hyperleukocytosis >100 ×10⁹/L.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003643.htm ; StatPearls NBK560882.

### 10. Platelets
- **Measures:** thrombocyte count.
- **Units:** /µL (SI ×10⁹/L).
- **Reference (CONFLICT on upper bound — both listed):** MedlinePlus **150–400 ×10⁹/L** (high ≥400) vs StatPearls **150–450 ×10⁹/L** (thrombocytosis >450 — current hematology practice).
- **Thrombocytopenia (StatPearls):** <150; mild 100–150 · moderate 50–100 · severe <50 ×10⁹/L; <20 spontaneous bleeding; <10 highest risk. MedlinePlus: bleeding risk higher <50.
- **Sources:** https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/003647.htm ; StatPearls NBK542208, NBK560810.
