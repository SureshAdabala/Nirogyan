# External Medical Research — Cardiovascular + Metabolic Domains
> Research pass: 2026-08-18. All values verified against fetched page content of authoritative sources (MedlinePlus/NIH, StatPearls/NCBI, ADA, NIDDK, PLOS Medicine, Cleveland Clinic secondary). Fetch-blocked primaries (AHA journals, CDC, Mayo) are cited as provenance only, with values verified via NIH-family mirrors.

## CARDIOVASCULAR DOMAIN

### 1. Total Cholesterol (TC)
- **Measures:** total cholesterol across LDL/HDL/other lipoprotein fractions.
- **Why it matters:** elevated TC drives atherosclerosis → CAD, MI, stroke risk.
- **Units:** mg/dL (SI: mmol/L, ×0.02586).
- **Reference (verified):** healthy adults 20+: **<200 mg/dL** (MedlinePlus). Not sex-specific.
- **Categories (NCEP ATP III legacy, verified via Cleveland Clinic):** Desirable <200 · Borderline high 200–239 · High ≥240 mg/dL.
- **Sources:**
  - MedlinePlus, "Cholesterol Levels: What You Need to Know" — https://medlineplus.gov/cholesterollevelswhatyouneedtoknow.html
  - MedlinePlus, "Cholesterol Levels" (lab test) — https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/cholesterol-levels/ (updated 2024-10-15)
  - Cleveland Clinic, "Cholesterol Numbers" — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/11920-cholesterol-numbers-what-do-they-mean

### 2. HDL Cholesterol (HDL-C)
- **Measures:** cholesterol carried by high-density lipoproteins (reverse transport to liver).
- **Why it matters:** higher HDL is protective; low HDL is an independent CV risk factor.
- **Units:** mg/dL (SI: mmol/L ×0.02586).
- **Reference (verified, MedlinePlus, sex-specific):** Men 20+: healthy **>40 mg/dL**; Women 20+: healthy **>50 mg/dL**; **≥60 mg/dL best** (both sexes).
- **Categories:** Low(M) <40 · Low(F) <50 · Acceptable above those · Optimal ≥60.
- **CONFLICT (recorded, not resolved):** StatPearls "Hypercholesterolemia" (NBK459188) uses female low-HDL cutoff **<55 mg/dL** vs MedlinePlus/NCEP **<50**. MedlinePlus matches NCEP/metabolic-syndrome convention.
- **Sources:**
  - MedlinePlus, "HDL: The 'Good' Cholesterol" — https://medlineplus.gov/hdlthegoodcholesterol.html
  - Cleveland Clinic (same page as TC); StatPearls NBK459188 — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK459188/

### 3. LDL Cholesterol (LDL-C)
- **Measures:** cholesterol carried by low-density lipoproteins.
- **Why it matters:** primary atherogenic particle; lower is better.
- **Units:** mg/dL (SI ×0.02586).
- **Reference (verified):** adults 20+: **<100 mg/dL** healthy (MedlinePlus).
- **Categories (NCEP ATP III, verified verbatim on MedlinePlus):** Optimal <100 · Near-optimal 100–129 · Borderline high 130–159 · High 160–189 · Very high ≥190 mg/dL.
- **Treatment-context (StatPearls):** high if >190, or >160 + 1 risk factor, or >130 + 2 risk factors; ASCVD goal <70 or ≥50% reduction.
- **Sources:** MedlinePlus "LDL: The Bad Cholesterol" — https://medlineplus.gov/ldlthebadcholesterol.html ; StatPearls NBK459188; Cleveland Clinic.

### 4. Triglycerides (TG, fasting)
- **Measures:** most common blood fat.
- **Why it matters:** high TG raises CAD risk; >1000 mg/dL risks pancreatitis (StatPearls).
- **Units:** mg/dL (SI: mmol/L ×0.01129). Fasting 9–12h (MedlinePlus).
- **Categories (verified):** Normal <150 · Borderline high 150–199 · High 200–499 · Very high ≥500 mg/dL. Not sex-specific.
- **Sources:** MedlinePlus "Triglycerides" — https://medlineplus.gov/triglycerides.html ; "Triglycerides Test" — https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/triglycerides-test/

### 5. Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP)
- **Measures:** arterial pressure at heart contraction ("top number").
- **Why it matters:** leading modifiable risk factor for MI, stroke, HF, CKD.
- **Units:** mmHg.
- **Categories (2017 ACC/AHA, verified via MedlinePlus + StatPearls; retained unchanged by the 2025 AHA/ACC guideline):**
  - Normal: SBP <120 AND DBP <80
  - Elevated: SBP 120–129 AND DBP <80
  - Stage 1 HTN: SBP 130–139 OR DBP 80–89
  - Stage 2 HTN: SBP ≥140 OR DBP ≥90
  - Hypertensive crisis: >180 and/or >120
- **Sources:**
  - MedlinePlus "High Blood Pressure" — https://medlineplus.gov/highbloodpressure.html
  - StatPearls "Essential Hypertension" — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK539859/
  - Provenance: Whelton 2017 ACC/AHA (ahajournals 403-blocked); 2025 confirmation via https://www.jacc.org/guidelines/high-blood-pressure

### 6. hs-CRP
- **Measures:** low-level CRP via high-sensitivity assay; CV inflammation marker.
- **Why it matters:** low-grade vascular inflammation predicts ASCVD events.
- **Units:** mg/L (⚠ routine CRP often mg/dL; 1 mg/dL = 10 mg/L).
- **CV risk categories (AHA/CDC 2003 statement values, verified via StatPearls):** Low <1 · Average 1–3 · High >3 mg/L.
- **Sources:**
  - StatPearls "C Reactive Protein" (upd. 2025-05-03) — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK441843/
  - MedlinePlus "C-Reactive Protein (CRP) Test" — https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/c-reactive-protein-crp-test/
  - Provenance: Pearson et al., Circulation 2003;107:499–511 (403-blocked).

### 7. ApoB:ApoA-I Ratio
- **Measures:** atherogenic particles (ApoB: one per LDL/VLDL/IDL) vs anti-atherogenic capacity (ApoA-I on HDL). Unitless.
- **Why it matters:** higher ratio → more plaque-depositing particles; predicts MACE decades ahead (AMORIS cohort, n=137,100).
- **NO US guideline categories → research-derived bands only (verified, PLOS Medicine/AMORIS 2021):** Low 0.2–0.6 · Medium 0.61–0.9 · High 0.91–5.0. No sex split in that paper.
- **Component ranges (Cleveland Clinic, verified):** ApoB M 66–133, F 60–117 mg/dL; ApoB >130 = higher risk.
- **Unverified (search-only, do not hard-code):** INTERHEART top-decile ≈1.28 OR>4; Walldius review ≥0.9 M / ≥0.8 F unfavorable.
- **STATUS: Needs Clarification** for guideline-grade categories — bands must be labeled research-derived (AMORIS), pending Niro's own sheet values.
- **Sources:**
  - Walldius et al., PLOS Medicine 2021;18(12):e1003853 — https://journals.plos.org/plosmedicine/article?id=10.1371/journal.pmed.1003853
  - Cleveland Clinic "Apolipoprotein B Test" — https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/diagnostics/24992-apolipoprotein-b-test

## METABOLIC DOMAIN

### 8. Fasting Glucose (FPG)
- **Measures:** blood glucose after ≥8h fast.
- **Why it matters:** ADA diagnostic test for prediabetes/diabetes.
- **Units:** mg/dL (126 = 7.0 mmol/L; 200 = 11.1 — verified via StatPearls).
- **ADA categories (verified on diabetes.org):** Normal <100 · Prediabetes 100–125 · Diabetes ≥126 mg/dL. (OGTT 2h: <140 / 140–199 / ≥200.)
- **Sources:** ADA "Diagnosis" — https://diabetes.org/about-diabetes/diagnosis ; StatPearls "Diabetes" — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK551501/

### 9. HbA1c
- **Measures:** % glycated hemoglobin ≈ 3-month average glucose.
- **Why it matters:** diagnosis + long-term glycemic control standard.
- **Units:** % (NGSP).
- **ADA categories (verified ADA + NIDDK, identical):** Normal <5.7 · Prediabetes 5.7–6.4 · Diabetes ≥6.5 %.
- **Sources:** ADA diagnosis page; NIDDK "The A1C Test & Diabetes" — https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/diagnostic-tests/a1c-test

### 10. Fasting Insulin
- **Measures:** serum insulin after 8–12h fast; used for hypoglycemia workup, insulin resistance (HOMA-IR input).
- **Units:** µIU/mL (= mIU/L).
- **STATUS: Needs Clarification — no universal guideline range.** MedlinePlus gives NO numeric range; assays not standardized. URMC (academic secondary): free insulin "<17 mcU/mL" with lab-variation caveat. Commercial-lab ranges (2.6–24.9 µIU/mL etc.) NOT verified.
- **Sources:** MedlinePlus "Insulin in Blood" — https://medlineplus.gov/lab-tests/insulin-in-blood/ ; URMC Health Encyclopedia "Total and Free Insulin (Blood)" — https://www.urmc.rochester.edu/encyclopedia/content?contenttypeid=167&contentid=insulin_total_free ; StatPearls "Insulin Resistance" — https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK507839/

### 11. Estimated Average Glucose (eAG)
- **DERIVED value** — no independent categories; interpret via HbA1c.
- **Formula (verified verbatim, ADA professional page):** **eAG = 28.7 × A1C − 46.7** (mg/dL). Basis: Nathan et al. ADAG Study, Diabetes Care 2008;31:1473–78.
- **Verified conversion anchors:** 6.0%→126 · 7.0%→154 · 8.0%→183 · 9.0%→212 · 10.0%→240 mg/dL.
- **Source:** ADA DiabetesPro "eAG/A1C Conversion Calculator" — https://professional.diabetes.org/glucose_calc
